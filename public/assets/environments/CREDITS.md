## Modifications (CYBERVERSE edition)

Generated by `tools/env-build/build-kit.mjs` from the untouched originals:

- only the pieces listed in `tools/env-build/pieces.json` are shipped; geometry only, original material names kept;
- materials are re-created at runtime (`src/world/materials.ts`): navy steel, ice ceramic, cyan light emitters, electric-blue accents driven by the MegaKit accent and emission masks;
- textures resized to 2K and 1K and converted to WebP. The base-colour maps are the neutral (non-red) variants. The mask maps are recombined: R = light emitters, G = accent paint;
- in-memory fix of a MegaKit defect: `Decal_Line_90.gltf` references a missing `.bin` file. The source file was not modified.

All other environment elements (island deck and hull, sky, cloud sea, holograms, portals, distant districts, data streams) are procedural geometry and shaders written for CYBERVERSE.
