# CYBERVERSE — Character credits and licences

Both characters are **modified editions** of VRoid Hub models. The original downloads are kept, unchanged, outside the
web root (`assets-source/04-characters-candidates/`, git-ignored). The production copies below were rebuilt from them
with `tools/char-lab/studio` (recipes `cyber-jt.js` and `neon-fighter.js`).

- Licence (VRoid Hub conditions, also embedded in the file): use by everyone; personal commercial use (profit) and
  corporate commercial use **allowed**; **modification allowed**; **redistribution allowed**; credit not required;
  **violent expression NOT allowed; sexual expression NOT allowed**.
- Modifications: hoodie and jeans redesigned as a techwear shell jacket and technical cargo trousers (new colour
  textures and emission maps: navy palette, yoke panel, cyan / violet light piping, centre zip, chest emblem, back
  circuit, sleeve stripes, strap, cargo pocket, knee panel, belt buckle), sneakers and glasses restyled (cyan-tinted
  lenses), shared MToon look; textures re-encoded to WebP, morph targets stored as sparse accessors (lossless).
  Face, hair, body proportions and skeleton unchanged.
