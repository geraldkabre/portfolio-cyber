# Study in China – Guidance Platform for Burkinabè Students

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | In progress |
| **Domain** | Web development · Education |

## Problem
Many students in Burkina Faso want to study in China, but reliable information is scattered: universities, scholarships, application steps, visa procedures and student life.

## Objective
Launch a web platform that offers reliable guidance and application resources to students, particularly from Burkina Faso, who wish to study in China.

## Planned Content
- University and programme directory
- Scholarship opportunities and deadlines
- Step-by-step application and visa guides
- Practical information on student life in China
